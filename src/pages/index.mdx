#### Something to Geek Out On

<aside class="bg-surface-container-lowest p-6 rounded-lg border border-white/[0.02] font-mono text-sm leading-relaxed mb-10">

* My resume is written in [JSON Schema format](https://jsonresume.org/). See [resume.clintp.json](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/yuhonas/clintp.xyz/main/resume/resume.clintp.json)
* Explore the resume data using visualization tools: [jsoncrack.com](https://jsoncrack.com/editor?json=https://raw.githubusercontent.com/yuhonas/clintp.xyz/main/resume/resume.clintp.json) or [lite.datasette.io](https://lite.datasette.io/?json=https://raw.githubusercontent.com/yuhonas/clintp.xyz/main/resume/resume.clintp.json#/data/resume?_sort=rowid&_facet=name&_facet=location)
* There is a [data transformation pipeline](https://github.com/yuhonas/clintp.xyz/blob/main/resume/resume-transform.py) written in [Spotify's Luigi](https://github.com/spotify/luigi) that produces a number of [artifacts](https://github.com/yuhonas/clintp.xyz/tree/main/resume/build) from my resume data, like a career timeline for [Timeline JS](https://cdn.knightlab.com/libs/timeline3/latest/embed/index.html?source=https://raw.githubusercontent.com/yuhonas/clintp.xyz/main/resume/build/timeline.json&font=Default&lang=en&hash_bookmark=true&initial_zoom=2&height=650#event-co-owner) and a contact [QR Code](https://github.com/yuhonas/clintp.xyz/blob/main/resume/build/clintp-qrcode.gif)
* As part of the [resume build](https://github.com/yuhonas/clintp.xyz/blob/main/.github/workflows/ci.yml), a gist is updated which enables the resume to be published on [registry.jsonresume.com](https://registry.jsonresume.org/yuhonas) (e.g. [stackoverflow theme](https://registry.jsonresume.org/yuhonas?theme=stackoverflow) or [lucide theme](https://registry.jsonresume.org/yuhonas?theme=lucide)).
* This site is [E2E tested](https://github.com/yuhonas/clintp.xyz/blob/main/cypress/e2e/site.cy.js) to ensure all the good stuff happens and the bad stuff doesn't.
* There is some sprinkling of AI primarily to supplement ideation and [design implementation](https://github.com/yuhonas/clintp.xyz/pull/117/changes) see [AGENT.md](https://github.com/yuhonas/clintp.xyz/blob/main/AGENTS.md) and [DESIGN-SYSTEM.md](https://github.com/yuhonas/clintp.xyz/blob/main/.ai/design-system.md)

</aside>
